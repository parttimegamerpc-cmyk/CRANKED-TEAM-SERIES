01 / Event structure

Win together.
Rank together.

It is not only about having one fast player. The Team Series rewards teams that plan their lineups, keep their roster active, and deliver across both Trails.

01 / Event cycle

A week to set your best

Depending on the season calendar, events may be weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. Each event cycle gives teams one full week to set their top leaderboard times.

02 / Two Trails

Trail A + Trail B

Every cycle features two designated map Trails. The team manager assigns one player to Trail A and a different player to Trail B.

03 / Roster rule

Depth makes the difference

A single player cannot run both Trails during the same event week. Choose your lineup carefully and bring your whole team into the competition.

02 / Your route to the leaderboard

How to compete.

Get your team ready before the first official Trails are announced. Check the event information for each cycle so your roster knows the plan.

01

Organise your team

Get your roster and team manager ready to coordinate the event lineup.

02

Assign two racers

Choose one player for Trail A and a different player for Trail B. No double-running both Trails in the same week.

03

Set your best times

Run the designated Trails within the event's one-week scoring window and aim for the strongest leaderboard rank possible.

04

Combine your points

Once results are verified, Trail A and Trail B points are added together to make your team's final score for the cycle.
